One letter for your day: **Z** — for **zombie**.

A day with zero sleep, walking through every hour like the living
dead. Vivid, funny, and honest: anyone who has had a sleepless day
recognises the shuffle at once. The letter fits its word — Z is the
initial of "zombie" and of "zero sleep", and its zig-zag shape even
looks like the walk of someone running on no sleep.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node, no dependencies) writes exactly one character,
`Z`, with no trailing newline, to
`(process.env.OUTPUT_DIR ?? "/output") + "/entry.txt"`.
